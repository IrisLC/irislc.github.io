Trailblazers, Fall 2025

World Building, Puzzle Design

The story world I created for the second time I took a Writing for Games/World Building class. The video provided is a mock pitch containing:

  1. A trailer for the world
  2. An overview of the first story that would be told in the world
  3. A trailer for a game prototype we made in the world

I personally spent a lot of time trying to make the first trailer akin to something that one may find in real marketing campaigns (obviously not in terms of quality, but at least in terms of vision). As for the game I made, it was a short ARG, giving the players the experience of breaking into the secrets of this shadowy organization which presents itself as a benevolent force in the world.

The ARG itself is quite simple, but if I had more time to work on it I would have loved to make a number of different websites and hidden videos and articles, asking the players to dive into source code, and audio manipulation to find the next piece of the puzzle. (A link to the start of the ARG can be found near the end of the video)

Also, for this class we had to make a number of different games in this story world all in different mediums. From a card game, to a TTRPG, to a Choose Your Own Adventure game made with Twine, to a game in GB Studio

Any and all assets used in the creation of the Trailers or ARG are listed at the end of the pitch.
