Castle Kitbash, Spring 2024

Unreal Engine

A kitbash project for an introduction Game Development class. Tasked with making a castle scene from a set of pre-made assets. Additionally, this was my first experience with utilizing a camera not attached to a player in Unreal Engine 5.

In creating this project I wanted to focus on the little details. The boats on the water, a cart bringing goods to market, and especially the stands set up inside the castle walls, with most of my time going towards making the items in the stands feel like a real human had set them down, with some things overflowing, boxes tilted on their side, hanging meat, etc. Unfortunately I ran out of time before I could turn my focus towards the town outside the castle, which I initially envisioned as a much larger part of the project, as that way we could see the place where the vendors who are selling goods in the castle live.
