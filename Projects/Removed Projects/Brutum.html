Monster Model, Spring 2025

A 3D model for a creature I took from concept to fully modeled, textured, and animated model for a video game.
This creature is meant to be a monster that can be found in the Trailblazers story world. While this model was used in multiple class projects, the model itself was not made for any specific project, and was instead something I decided to devote my personal time to learning how to do as I realized one of the areas in which I lacked much skill was 3d modeling and animation.

Provided are two screenshots from blender, showing the model with a realistic render engine, sa well as a video showcasing two of the four created animations for the creature, that being a spawn animation/cutscene and kill animation for if the player is caught in a chase. Not shown is a walk cycle and death animation/cutscene showcasing the monster falling out a window.
