About Me

My name is Iris Currie. I am a Games + Simulations major focusing on Game development and programming, with a minor in Computer Science, With a planned graduation date from Miami University with my bachelor's degree in the Spring of 2027.

I have grown up playing video games (some of my favorites being Destiny 2, Omori, Hollow Knight, Titanfall 2 and Fallout: New Vegas), and it has been a long time dream of mine to make them, but for many years that's all I thought it could be, a dream, as I spent my time focusing on theater and acting. But now that I have changed my focuses to the art of creating games, I am striving to make that old dream a reality. In my three years since making that shift I have devoted myself to make up for this lost time.Throughout my time at Miami I have been taking as many classes as I can to learn every facet of the gaming industry, from programming, to design, music, art, story telling, and whatever else I can get my hands on. Meanwhile when not in these classes or doing research by playing more games, I've been taking what time I can to work on personal projects, engage with game jams, and expanding my knowledge base and skill sets wherever possible beyond the classroom.


Additionally, I spend my free time at college participating in extracurriculars. Those being the Miami University Game Design Club, Guitar Club, for a time serving as an officer on the University's Fighting game club, and formerly as a member of the Miami University Varsity Street Fighter 6 team.
